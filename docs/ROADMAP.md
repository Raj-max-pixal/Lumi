## MVP vs Advanced

### MVP
- auth
- feed
- posts
- reels
- stories
- chat
- profiles
- explore
- jobs basic
- AI captions/replies/moderation

### Advanced
- live streaming backend
- full recommendations engine
- E2E encryption
- screenshot detection
- subscriptions/tips/revenue share
- creator studio
- large-scale analytics
- native camera/filter stack
